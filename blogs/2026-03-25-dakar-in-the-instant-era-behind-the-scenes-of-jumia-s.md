Dakar is a city of contrasts, where the bustling energy of the Sandaga markets meets the modernity of the new urban hub in Diamniadio. Yet, for every resident of the Senegalese capital, one reality remains: the complexity of getting around. It is within this context of logistical challenges that Jumia Senegal launched its most ambitious project to date in late January 2026: Jumia Instant.
