The e-commerce landscape in Egypt continues to evolve, with customers increasingly looking for greater flexibility in how they shop and pay. One of the key developments shaping this evolution is the growing adoption of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) solutions. At Jumia Egypt, BNPL has become an increasingly important part of the shopping experience, supported by a large network of financing partners that gives customers more choice when purchasing products online.
